# Medical and safety note

Starholm's guides, its library and the assistant's answers are general information. They are not medical advice, and
no doctor or other health professional has reviewed them.

**In an emergency, call your local emergency number first.** Use Starholm while you wait for help, or when help
can't reach you.

- **Follow your national health authority.** When its advice or your local emergency services' advice differs from
  what you read in Starholm, follow theirs.
- **Check the page behind every answer.** The assistant can be wrong. It can misread a page, mix up two pages or leave
  out a step. Every answer shows the pages it used, so open them before you act on it.
- **The library is other people's text.** Its pages are copies of documents from the publishers listed in
  [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md). A page can be out of date or wrong, and a copy could have been changed on its way to
  your computer. Starholm checks downloads where the publisher gives a way to, but it can't promise that every page
  is correct.
- **Check every medicine dose** with a pharmacist, a doctor or the leaflet in the box. A child's dose depends on their
  age and weight.
- **Check the emergency numbers.** Each country card shows the date its numbers were checked against an official
  page. Numbers change, so confirm yours with your local authority.
- **Take a first-aid course.** Practice on a dummy with a trainer teaches what no page can.
